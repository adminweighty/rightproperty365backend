# Agent workspace guide

For estate agents, agencies, property managers, developers, hosts, solicitors and their staff, and for **landlords
and property sellers** who list their own property, as far as the same screens apply. Covers everything up to API
0.61.0. The workspace is at **`/agent`** on the website; the mobile app has the same areas except where noted.

## Getting started

1. **Register** with your email address and confirm it. Choose your role (for example Estate Agent, Agency Principal,
   Landlord, Property Seller). A property-seeker account cannot also be a professional one: use a separate account.
2. **Verification.** Upload the documents your role asks for, in **Onboarding**. We review them and email you. Until
   you are approved, nothing you list is public.
3. **Join or create your organisation.** An Agency Principal's approval creates the agency. Agents and staff either
   apply to join a named agency, or accept an invitation emailed by the agency.
4. **Sign-in** is two steps: your password, then a code we email you. You can see and sign out your devices, change
   your password and change your email address in **Account → Security**.

- **Branches.** If you are placed in a branch, you see only that branch's listings, requests and ledger entries, and
  those that belong to no branch. Another branch's work shows "This belongs to another branch".
- **Service providers** (for example cleaners, inspectors) receive enquiries and consultations but do not list
  property.
- **Landlords and property sellers** work alone. They manage their own listings, see only requests for their own
  listings, and have their own contact book, books and properties. Team, branch and assignment features do not apply.
- Nobody can change or remove an owner, and nobody can change their own membership.

## Listings

- **Create:** **Listings → New**. Fill in the basics, price, location (exact or approximate on the public map),
  features and description.
- **Photos:** add up to 20; describe each one for people who cannot see it. Photos are resized and their camera and
  location data removed.
- **YouTube tour:** paste a video link.
- **Send for review:** our team checks every new listing and every change to a public one. Until approved, visitors
  see the approved version.
- **Publication** lasts **90 days**. We remind you 14, 7 and 1 days before it ends. Then it is no longer public. To
  publish it for another 90 days, **Relist** it. Renewing your subscription does not relist anything.
- **While public:** **Pause** and **Resume**, **Mark as sold or let**, **Close**, **Archive**. A listing that was never
  published can be deleted.
- **Your package** sets how many listings can be public at once; the editor warns when it is full.
- **Short stays:** set nights, prices and the availability calendar; guests send requests to stay (no instant
  booking).
- **Hourly venues** (showrooms, halls, studios, offices): set an hourly rate, opening hours, minimum and maximum
  duration, cleaning buffer, deposit and fees. Customers get a quote and send a request; you confirm or decline.
  Payment is arranged between you and the customer.
- **Featured or sponsored:** placements are given by our team under your subscription. The listing then shows a
  label and a disclosure line.

## Requests from customers (the inbox)

- **Requests** lists enquiries, information requests, viewing and consultation requests. Every request comes
  through RightProperty365, which you can trust: you never get a customer's details by any other route.
- Open a request, then:
  - **move it through stages:** New → Assigned → Contacted → Viewing requested or scheduled → Converted, or
    Follow-up required, or Closed;
  - **assign** it (owners and managers);
  - **reply** in the conversation (the customer sees your replies without your contact details);
  - **add private notes**;
  - **create a contact** from it.
- If a customer came from an advertising campaign, the request says which one.
- **Requests → Funnel:** how requests move through the stages, response times, and breakdowns by agent, request
  type, listing and campaign. A spreadsheet download is available.

## Appointments

- **Availability:** your working hours per day, time zone, buffers, lead time, holidays, and the **consultation
  services** you offer (free; duration, online or in person). Customers then pick from your free slots.
- **Appointments:** confirm, propose another time, cancel, mark completed or no-show. Both sides get emails and a
  calendar file, and reminders before the time. Two requests cannot both be confirmed for the same slot.

## Contacts (CRM)

- **Contacts:** your organisation's private address book. Add, tag, classify (buyer, tenant, landlord, owner), link to
  listings, add notes and reminders, and record marketing permission.
- **Import** and **export** spreadsheets, and **merge** duplicates (notes and history are kept).
- **Pipeline:** contacts by lead stage.
- Another organisation can never see your contacts.

## Questionnaires and applications

- **Forms:** build reusable application questionnaires (text, choices, numbers, dates, files, conditions). Publish a
  version and attach it to listings; open or close intake.
- **Applications:** review, request more information, shortlist, accept or reject. Each application keeps exactly
  the questions it was submitted with. Opening an application or its files is recorded.

## Books (ledger)

- Record income, expenses, rent, commissions and deposits per currency, with attachments and notes.
- Entries are never edited after posting: **reverse** or **correct** them, and the history stays.
- Entries you record yourself are marked "recorded by you, not verified". Mark entries **reconciled** when you have
  checked them against your bank.
- **Reports:** cashbook, ledger detail, arrears and statements, always per currency. A spreadsheet download is
  available.

## Property management

- **Properties**, units and **tenancies**: rent schedules are created, then you record receipts; **Arrears** lists
  what is overdue.
- **Maintenance** requests (tenants can report them with photos) and **Inspections** with photos per item.
- **Leases:** design your own **lease templates** (they are yours; we do not provide legal wording), generate a DRAFT
  lease from a tenancy, and send it for **electronic signing**. Signers get an emailed code, can adopt a typed
  signature, and your organisation's **digital stamp** (text you type) can be applied. A completed agreement gets an
  audit report, and anyone can check a copy's fingerprint. This is not a notarised or registered signature.

## Developments

**Developments:** showcase a project with phases, unit types, photos and linked listings. It is reviewed before it
is public. Never state guaranteed returns.

## Analytics

**Analytics:** impressions, detail views, unique views, contact clicks, enquiries, applications and viewings, per
listing and over time (24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or custom). The definitions are on `/analytics-definitions`. Figures
will not match other tools exactly.

## Team

**Team:**
- invite people by email, change roles, suspend or remove members, and approve or decline applications to join;
- the **Activity** view shows every change (owners and managers);
- **Branches:** add, rename or archive branches, and place people in one;
- tie a listing to a branch on the listing's page;
- when someone leaves, their open requests and appointments go back to the organisation inbox.

## Your account and subscription

- **Subscription and invoices:** see your plan, invoices and receipts. Online payment is not switched on yet: pay by
  the method on the invoice.
- **Notifications:** choose which non-essential emails and app notifications you get. Account and security messages
  are always sent.
- **Download your data:** request a copy (ZIP). We email you when it is ready.
- **Delete your account:** **Account → Delete**, with your password. The only owner of an organisation that still
  has members must hand over or remove them first.

## On the phone

The mobile app covers:
- listings, the inbox and replies, contacts, appointments and availability;
- statistics, team and branches;
- books, properties, rent, maintenance and inspections;
- leases and signing, invoices (read-only), notifications and account settings.

Its limits:
- Push notifications and opening website links in the app are **not switched on yet**.
- Use the website for the funnel spreadsheet, questionnaire building, contact import and merge, and the admin area.
